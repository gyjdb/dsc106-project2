# DSC 106 Project 2: Persuasive or Deceptive Visualization?

Live site: https://gyjdb.github.io/dsc106-project2/

Proposition: "China is beating the U.S. in the tech race."

The final has two visualizations, one for the proposition and one against it, each with 3–5 scored design decisions.

## Files

- `index.html`: the report page
- `C1_growth_rates.png`: C1, for the proposition (S2 at the checkpoint)
- `U1_IP_Income.png`: U1, against the proposition

## How C1 is calculated

Compound annual growth from 2000 to the latest year both countries report: patents (resident applications) 2019, research papers 2018, R&D spending 2018, IP royalties 2020. High-tech exports run 2007–2020. R&D spending is R&D's share of GDP multiplied by GDP at PPP (current international $), both from the course's World Bank snapshot (September 2021).
